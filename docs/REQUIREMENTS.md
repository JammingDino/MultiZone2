## Status

This document is a stub. It should be written in full around the 0.4.x release cycle, once the architecture is stable and contributor onboarding becomes a real need. Writing it earlier risks documenting decisions that are still in flux.

When it is written, it should cover:

- **Personas** — who uses the app and what they are trying to accomplish (e.g. solo researcher, developer, power user running local models, non-technical user with cloud providers)
- **User stories** — concrete scenarios per persona that drive feature decisions
- **Functional requirements** — what the app must do, stated as requirements not implementation
- **Non-functional requirements** — performance, privacy, portability, security, robustness
- **Out of scope** — explicit list of things the app will not do (important for saying no clearly)

The competitive analysis in [COMPETITORS.md](COMPETITORS.md) is a good starting point for understanding what the landscape expects and where MultiZone draws its lines.
